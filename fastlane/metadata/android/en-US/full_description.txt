Aura Music is an offline folder-based music player for Android with a dark OLED interface and its own A·Play design.

Every subfolder inside Music/AuraMusic automatically becomes a playlist, with full support for emojis in folder names. Keep your music organized in plain folders on your device—no cloud accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

Features:
• Folder-based library: Create playlists as subfolders from the app, pick another root folder, and rescan anytime.
• Home & quick access: 3x3 quick-access grid (Liked Music, playlists, Recently Added, Most Played, and artist mixes), Recently Played strip, and playlist chips.
• Instant search: Search songs, artists, folders, and playlists ignoring accents and case, with saved recent searches.
• Library & Songs views: Switch between list and grid layouts, sort playlists by name or song count, filter songs by folder, and sort by title, artist, or duration.
• Playlist view & Liked Music: Full-width 2x2 cover collage, play, shuffle, add to queue, and persistent Liked Music with folder filters.
• Dynamic player: Background gradient extracted from each track's cover art, Like and Save actions, shuffle, repeat / repeat one, seek bar, and Up Next queue management.
• Synchronized lyrics: Reads local .lrc files beside the audio or fetches synced lyrics from LRCLIB (no account required) and caches them for offline use. Tap any verse to seek.
• Add from YouTube: Paste a YouTube song or public/unlisted playlist link, choose the destination folder, and download .m4a audio through a FIFO queue with progress, cancel, retry, and automatic cover letterbox cropping.
• Background playback: MediaSession notification with cover art, progress bar, repeat toggle, and like button.
• Filename parsing: Extracts artist and title from filenames like "Artist - Title (Official Video) ft. X" when tags are missing.

Privacy:
No accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Network access is used only when you paste a YouTube link, look up lyrics on LRCLIB, or restore the missing cover of a song downloaded from YouTube.
